Each round recruits eight to twelve participants across visual, motor, cognitive and hearing needs. Sessions are run remotely with assistive technology the participant already uses. Findings are triaged into the release backlog, and a retest is booked before sign-off. The research panel includes two switch-access users and three screen-reader users.
