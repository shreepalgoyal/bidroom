The extract covers service demand, transaction volumes, case outcomes and response times at a granularity that cannot identify a resident. It is generated by a scheduled job on the first working day of the month, validated against the published schema and pushed to a stable URL with a versioned filename. The Open Government Licence notice is embedded in the dataset metadata and on the download page.
