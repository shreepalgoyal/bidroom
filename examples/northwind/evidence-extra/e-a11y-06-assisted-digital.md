Every journey offers “finish this another way” at each step. The resident can request a callback, start a web chat with an adviser or book a face-to-face session at a library or council office. The adviser sees the same draft, so the resident does not repeat information. Advisers are trained on the top 20 transactions and on supporting residents with low digital confidence. The route is tested with disabled users in the annual accessibility review.
