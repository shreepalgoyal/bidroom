The portal is the only public entry point for council tax, housing, waste, parking, planning, benefits and adult social care. A resident signs in once and sees every open case, payment and request in one place. Internal teams publish through the same catalogue, so a new service inherits search, notifications, accessibility and analytics without a separate build.
